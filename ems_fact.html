Why the fact bank changed

Fast facts are useful only if they are dependable. Daily EMS Quiz now favors facts that affect assessment, testing, treatment priorities, or EMS systems knowledge. Claims are linked back to sources such as the National Registry, American Heart Association, CDC, and EMS.gov.

Clinical use: A daily fact is a memory prompt, not a complete protocol. Follow your education program, current service protocols, medical direction, and state scope of practice.
