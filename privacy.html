Privacy Policy for Daily EMS Quiz

Effective date: October 1, 2026

1. No Account Required

No login, name, email, or identifying information is required to use the quizzes. We may collect anonymous usage data to help improve the app. This does not include any personally identifiable information.

This app does not use third-party SDKs or advertising networks. If embedded content is added later, it will be disclosed and updated in this policy.

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under this age.
