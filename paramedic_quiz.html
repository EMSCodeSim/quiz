Current-care notes built into the bank

  • Oxygen is a treatment, not a ritual: suspected ACS does not automatically require oxygen when the patient is not hypoxemic.
  • COPD does not mean “withhold oxygen”: treat hypoxemia and ventilation problems while titrating therapy and monitoring the patient; the old “hypoxic drive” shortcut is not an adequate clinical explanation.
  • Unstable tachyarrhythmia is a perfusion problem: determine whether the rhythm is causing instability and act promptly when it is.
  • Local protocol still wins: drug doses, RSI pathways, sedation, destination policy, and scope vary by service.
