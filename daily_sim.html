Suggested practice order

VITAL SIGNS

Blood pressure + pulse

Practice the mechanics first, then document rate, rhythm, quality and pressure in a patient context.

NEURO

Pupils + stroke

Use findings as decision cues. A pupil finding or stroke deficit matters because it changes the differential, priority, or destination.

TRAUMA

Rule of Nines

Practice estimating burn surface area, then connect the estimate to severity, transport planning and local protocol.

Skill practice is not a protocol

The simulators are educational approximations designed to strengthen observation and assessment habits. Real equipment, patient anatomy, audio findings, local procedures and scope vary. Use your course, service equipment, and medical direction for competency validation.
