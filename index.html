Know what the exam actually weights

Current EMT content is assessment-heavy.

The National Registry’s updated EMT examination reorganized the old topic buckets into five practice-centered domains. Primary Assessment is the largest portion of the exam.

Scene Size-Up & Safety
15–19%

Hazards, resources, mechanism/nature of illness, and initial scene decisions.

Primary Assessment
39–43%

General impression, life threats, airway, breathing, circulation, and priority.

Secondary Assessment
5–9%

Focused history, physical exam, relevant vital signs, and findings.

Treatment & Transport
20–24%

Interventions, reassessment, destination, and transport decisions.

Operations
10–14%

EMS systems, communication, safety, incidents, and operations.

Source: National Registry certification examination content, reviewed August 10, 2026. Pediatric care is integrated throughout the domains. View the official NREMT examination content.

Daily EMS Quiz FAQ

Is Daily EMS Quiz free?

Yes. The EMT and Paramedic daily quizzes, Clinical Judgment Lab, study guide, sourced facts, medication review, terminology game, and core skills tools are available without a login.

Is this an official NREMT practice test?

No. Daily EMS Quiz is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Registry. “NREMT” is used descriptively when discussing the public certification examination blueprint.

Why only five quiz questions at a time?

The default set is meant to be easy to repeat. After submitting, you can run another vetted set or move into a scenario or skill drill based on what you missed.

Does this replace local EMS protocols?

No. Medication doses, scope of practice, procedures, destination rules, and operational policy vary. Follow your education program, medical direction, state scope, and local protocols.
