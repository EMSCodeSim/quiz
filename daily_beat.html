Counting shortcuts

15 secondsCount beats for 15 seconds and multiply by 4. Faster, but a single counting error has a bigger effect.
30 secondsCount for 30 seconds and multiply by 2. Gives more observation time for rate and regularity.
Irregular pulseThis trainer uses a regular pulse. In real patients, an irregular pulse deserves a longer count and appropriate cardiac assessment rather than a shortcut.
Rate is only one findingDocument rhythm/regularity and pulse quality when relevant, and interpret the rate with perfusion, mental status, symptoms and the rest of the patient.

What this simulator does—and does not do

The audio is a synthetic timing cue, not a recorded human pulse or ECG rhythm. It is designed only to train rate-counting mechanics. A real pulse assessment also includes location, strength, regularity, symmetry when relevant, and the patient’s overall perfusion.
